# Economy report

_Generated by `lune run tools/economy` from WoodData, ItemCatalog and GameConfig. Don't edit by hand: change the data or the assumptions in `tools/economy.luau`, then re-run._

**Model, not measurement.** Numbers assume trees are always available and nobody competes for them. "Real player" = the ideal model × EFFICIENCY (0.50), a guess until a playtest calibrates it.

## Key findings

- **Earning rates are within 25% of the design's targets.**
- **Weak axes stay on their own tier:** the Rusty Axe earns the most in the starter forest.
- **The whole progression (Inferno Axe + Logging Rig) takes 13.6 h of play** for a focused player.

## Calibrating with a playtest

The ideal model earns **$33/min** with the Rusty Axe and Rustbucket on birch. Play that loop in Studio for ~10 minutes and read the `[Stopwatch] ... overall` $/min in the Output window. Set `EFFICIENCY` in `tools/economy.luau` to your number ÷ $33, and re-run.

## Assumptions

- Swing every 0.8 s; carry 2 logs by hand (GameConfig).
- Walk 16 studs/s; every truck drives 26 studs/s; boats 26 studs/s.
- One-way haul to the sawmill (studs): starter 190, hills 400, snow 1200, volcano 1600, island 900, grove 800.
- 15 studs between trees, 20 studs from tree to truck, 0.5 s per prompt press, 8 s to get in/out and park.
- Volcano needs Heat Boots, the Phantom Grove needs a Lantern and only exists at night (left out of the path), the Island needs a boat, and snow is 15% slower without the Insulated Coat.
- Each load = cargo + a full armful; logs are sold at WoodData prices.
