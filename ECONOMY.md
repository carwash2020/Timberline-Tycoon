# Economy report

_Generated by `lune run tools/economy` from WoodData, ItemCatalog and GameConfig. Don't edit by hand: change the data or the assumptions in `tools/economy.luau`, then re-run._

**Model, not measurement.** Numbers assume trees are always available and nobody competes for them. "Real player" = the ideal model × EFFICIENCY (0.50), **a guess until a playtest calibrates it** (see below).

## Key findings

- **Earning rates are within 25% of the design's targets.**
- **Weak axes stay on their own tier:** the Rusty Axe earns the most in the starter forest.
- **The forge grind:** before the Starfall Axe, Lumenwood with the Inferno Axe earns $17,625/h (emberwood: $18,635/h). With the Starfall Axe it earns $30,013/h.
- **All of V1 (Inferno Axe, Logging Rig, forged Starfall Axe) takes 16.8 h of play** for a focused player.

## Calibrating with a playtest

1. In Studio, play the starter loop with the Rusty Axe and Rustbucket for about 10 minutes: chop, load the truck, sell at the mill.
2. The Output window prints a line starting `lune run tools/economy --calibrate` (every minute, and when you stop).
3. Run that line in the repo folder. It saves EFFICIENCY to `tools/calibration.json` and rewrites this report with honest numbers.

Current: **0.50 (a guess)**. For reference, the ideal model earns $33/min on birch with the starter kit.

## Assumptions

- Swing every 0.8 s; carry 2 logs by hand (GameConfig).
- Walk 16 studs/s; every truck drives 26 studs/s; boats 26 studs/s.
- One-way haul to the sawmill in studs, from BiomeData: Starter Forest 190 (truck), The Hills 400 (truck), Snowfields 1200 (truck), The Volcano 1600 (truck), The Island 900 (boat), Phantom Grove 800 (truck).
- Aether Isles: gondola 40 s each way ($250 a ride), logs carried 30 studs to the Cloud Chute, Sky Bin holds 30.
- Forged axes cost their cash plus the logs and materials given up (at sell value), plus 1 min per Sky Shard to mine.
- 15 studs between trees, 20 studs from tree to truck, 0.5 s per prompt press, 8 s to get in/out and park.
- Biome rules come from BiomeData: required gear (volcano: Heat Boots; Phantom Grove: Lantern, night only, so left out of the path), boats for the Island, and slowdowns (snow without the Insulated Coat).
- Each load = cargo + a full armful; logs are sold at WoodData prices.
