# Economy report

_Generated by `lune run tools/economy1` from WoodData, ItemCatalog and GameConfig. Don't edit by hand: change the data or the assumptions in `tools/economy1.luau`, then re-run._

**Model, not measurement.** Numbers assume trees are always available and nobody competes for them. "Real player" = the ideal model × EFFICIENCY (0.50), **a guess until a playtest calibrates it** (see below).

## Key findings

- **Earning runs about 1.3× faster than the design's targets** (median of the early, mid and end-game checks below).
- **The Living Forest's figured wood adds 6.1% to every wood** (the Hidden Grain's average at a Healthy grove, counted in every rate here; 6.7% on Lumenwood). See "The Living Forest" below.
- **Weak axes stay on their own tier:** the Rusty Axe earns the most in the starter forest.
- **The forge grind:** before the Starfall Axe, Lumenwood with the Inferno Axe earns $18,982/h (emberwood: $26,150/h). With the Starfall Axe it earns $32,324/h.
- **All of V1 (Inferno Axe, Logging Rig, forged Starfall Axe) takes 12.9 h of play** for a focused player.
- **The $2,000,000 wallet cap** (GameConfig.CashCap) takes 61.9 h of saving at the best V1 loop ($32,324/h), far past the whole progression, so it only stops hardcore grinders.

## Calibrating with a playtest

1. In Studio, play the starter loop with the Rusty Axe and Rustbucket for about 10 minutes: chop, load the truck, sell at the mill.
2. The Output window prints a line starting `lune run tools/economy1 --calibrate` (every minute, and when you stop).
3. Run that line in the repo folder. It saves EFFICIENCY to `tools/calibration.json` and rewrites this report with honest numbers.

Current: **0.50 (a guess)**. For reference, the ideal model earns $32/min on birch with the starter kit.

## The Living Forest

Some trees hide a figure that sells for more (ForestData): 6 in 100 at a Healthy grove, a figured tree's logs worth 2.02× on average (2.12× on Lumenwood, where 3 in 10 burls are Starfall Burls). Every rate above includes that average at a Healthy grove. Storm strikes, Elders, Skyroot Blooms and the planted-tree bonus are events on top and aren't counted.

What figures add to wood income, by the grove's vitality tier:

| Grove | Figure chance (wild / planted) | Adds (wild) | Adds (planted) | In a Skyroot Bloom (wild) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thinning (0+) | 6% / 6% | +6.1% | +6.1% | +12.2% |
| Healthy (35+) | 6% / 6% | +6.1% | +6.1% | +12.2% |
| Thriving (75+) | 6% / 6% | +6.1% | +6.1% | +12.2% |
| Old Growth (90+) | 6% / 6% | +6.1% | +6.1% | +12.2% |

Figure chance is flat at the Healthy rate. The grove meter and planting are not in the live loop, so a grove no longer drifts (the old rest was 60, 1 a minute).

## Assumptions

- Swing every 0.8 s; carry 2 logs by hand (GameConfig).
- Walk 16 studs/s; trucks drive at their top speed (Rustbucket 26, Timber Classic 30, Pickup 30, Scout ATV 40, Flatbed 26, Logging Rig 24 studs/s); boats 26 studs/s.
- One-way haul to the sawmill in studs, from BiomeData: Starter Forest 260 (truck), The Hills 420 (truck), Snowfields 650 (truck), The Volcano 700 (truck), The Island 900 (boat), Gloam Hollow 800 (truck), Bayou 1330 (truck), Red Mesa 1200 (truck).
- Aether Isles: gondola 40 s each way ($250 a ride), logs carried 30 studs to the Cloud Chute, Sky Bin holds 30.
- Forged axes cost their cash plus the logs and materials given up (at sell value), plus 1 min per Sky Shard to mine.
- 15 studs between trees, 20 studs from tree to truck, 0.5 s per prompt press, 8 s to get in/out and park.
- Biome rules come from BiomeData: required gear (volcano: Heat Boots; Phantom Grove: Lantern, night only, so left out of the path), boats for the Island, and slowdowns (snow without the Insulated Coat).
- Each load = cargo + a full armful; logs are sold at WoodData prices × the figure average at a Healthy grove (ForestData).
- Murph's tutorial pays $50 (TutorialData), counted toward the first purchase.
- The first plot costs $150 (GameConfig.PlotPrice, the Land Office) and is bought before the first upgrade; v1 has no Murph reward toward it.
- Daily goals (DailyData) aren't counted above: at most $105 a day with the Rusty Axe, up to $9,100 with the Starfall Axe (all goals plus a 5-day streak bonus).
