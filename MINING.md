# Mining income

_Generated by `lune run tools/mining_economy`. Don't edit by hand._

Mining $/h is the supply of **one chamber** (the mix in `MiningLogic.Mix`). A faster pick cannot outrun respawn. Net value is the better of the chunk and the ingot after the coal a smelt burns (`chunkValue / 4`). Comparison wood is ECONOMY.md's "$/h cap a player (shared by 4)" column: Hills Pine, Snowfields Frostwood (higher than Emberwood), Gloamwood. That is the biome supply cap, not the buying-path wage.

Cap: mining stays at or under **0.8×** that wood. The tool fails above it.
