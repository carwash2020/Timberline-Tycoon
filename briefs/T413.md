TITLE: T413 Roads, bridges and a car ferry replace the gondola. Players travel on purpose.

## Goal
Players reach Dale's Dealership over a paid lift bridge, reach Lantern Isle Sparkworks on a walkable car ferry, and reach the Skyroot GateLodge on a switchback road. The town/main bridge stays free. Junction signs point the way. No gondola returns. MeshId 0 greybox is fine until Connor uploads.

## Player story
"As a player I spawn and cross the town bridge with $0 spent. When I can afford a truck I pay $100 once at the out-of-town lift bridge, drive to Dale's lot, then return. Later I drive to the mainland ferry dock, board with my truck and trailer, pay $350 at cast-off, walk the deck for about 40 s, and drive off at Sparkworks harbour. I climb the Skyroot switchback to the GateLodge with no cash toll on the road. Signs say DEALERSHIP, FERRY / LANTERN ISLE, SKYROOT and TOWN (free bridge). I never see a gondola."
