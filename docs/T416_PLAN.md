# T416 look fixes and T415 proofs

One PR, branch `t416-look-fixes`, base main `c423050`. Goal: seven small look fixes the art review asked for, plus the before shots and counts that prove T415 (#287) changed no look. No economy, StoreData, ProfileSchema, remote or Vendor change. Palette names only (Kit.hex / Palette); nothing is added to Palette unless a name is missing (none is expected: SignCream, SignInk, TimberBrown, Brass, PlankHoney, StoneBase, TrailDirt, CliffRock, QuarrySlate, RoadGravel and Lantern all exist).

Every item: spec first, shown red on main, then green. No existing assertion is loosened silently; each changed one is listed in the PR body with the reason.

## Files and approach per item

0. **No keep-out overlay in the world.** The red keep-out strips (`Line` parts in a `KeepOuts` model) exist only in the preview helper `tools/preview/scenes/_t414d2.luau`; nothing under `src/` builds them. Add `tests/NoOverlay.spec.luau`: after the town build and the scatter build there is no Part or Model named `KeepOuts` / `KeepOut*` / `Overlay*` anywhere under `TimberlineMap`, and no source under `src/` creates one (source scan). The preview helper gets an `overlay` switch that defaults OFF (a `T414_OVERLAY=1` environment variable turns it on for a debug shot), and the #284 and #287 eye shots are reshot with it off into `preview/t416/`.
1. **Hanging boards in Hearth & Home and the Tool Shed** (`Art/ShopKit.luau` `hung` / `HeaderSign` / `SectionSigns`, called by `ShopInteriors/HearthAndHome.luau` and `ToolShed.luau`). A `house` flag on the sign spec (set by those two shops only; Sparkworks and the Dealership keep their boards) draws the house sign style: SignCream board, SignInk text on the board's own faces (SurfaceGui on the board part, both faces), a TimberBrown frame (top rail and the under-rail that used to be the category-coloured accent bar, which is the blue under BUILD PLANS and HOME PLANS), Brass brackets where the chains meet the board. Names (`HeaderSign`, `HeaderSignFrame`, `HeaderSignChain`), the `SignText` attribute and chain count stay. Spec `ShopBoards.spec`: every HeaderSign in both shops has SignCream board, SignInk text, TimberBrown frame, Brass brackets; no board part (board, frame, accent, bracket, chain) is blue (hue 190 to 260 degrees and clearly saturated); text label sits on a face of the board it belongs to; names and attributes the code reads.
2. **Heat Boots and Secret Shop** (`Art/ShopFinish.luau`, booth finish). Each booth already has two back-wall honey `WallShelf` boards; add a wall shelf run on each side wall (WoodPlanks, PlankHoney, `WallShelfRun`) clear of the 8-wide walk and the 3-stud shoulders, and one `Lantern` pendant (a chain, a body in the Lantern colour #FFC45C and a small PointLight) that leaves 10 studs of clear height under it. `ShopBounds` semantics are not changed. Extend `ShopInterior.spec` (new tests, existing ones untouched) to both shops: walk, shoulders, door clear of the new parts, 10 studs clear height under the lantern, shelf material and colour, exactly one lantern each.
3. **Plot pad edge** (`Shared/PlotPlatform.luau`). Remove the sloped `Ramp` wedge and the wedge curb. The edge becomes a StoneBase `Curb` block whose top is flush with the pad top, then flat TrailDirt `Rim` treads. The pad is 1 stud above the grass (Connor's 7 October lock), so the drop is taken in treads of at most 0.25 each (curb to grass: every riser at most 0.25), no part is a wedge. Layout, slab rects, `Origin` and saved plots are unchanged. `PlotPlatform.spec`: the tests that pinned the slope (`ramp's high edge ... low edge`, `rise follows the ramp's slope`, `no coplanar overlap` of ramps, `edge is a StoneBase curb over a TrailDirt rim`, `MaxLip`) are adapted to the new parts only as required and listed in the PR body. New assertions: no WedgePart in the edge, curb top flush with the pad top, every riser at most 0.25, the rim reaches the grass inside `PlatformRamp`.
4. **Mining nodes** (`Art/NodeArt.luau`). Replace the ellipsoid boulders with clusters of rotated, faceted WedgePart pairs and tilted blocks, Slate material, CliffRock / QuarrySlate, no bare cube and no sphere. Amber / Garnet veins stay (their anchors are fitted to the faceted bodies). Footprint, hit box, attributes and payouts untouched (`NodeService.spec` unchanged). `NodeArt.spec`: no SpecialMesh sphere, no unrotated block, every rock a wedge or a block turned off axis on two axes, material Slate, colours from the two palette names, veins kept, still inside the footprint.
5. **Decor stumps** (`Art/Greenery.luau` `Stump`). Top = the species core hex (`TreeArt.FaceLook`), sides = bark; pale bark (birch, grey-white) is darkened so no stump part reads white. Frostwood (core #E4F2FA, near white) is dropped from the snow region's stump species (pine only). `Greenery` / `ScatterWorld` spec: for every species a decor stump can use, the cut face equals the species core hex and no stump part colour is white or near white (luma over 0.9 or every channel over 0.92).
6. **Snow road** (`Art/TravelArt.luau` `SkyrootRoad`). The terrain road in the snow region is already Asphalt (measured: the whole SnowRoad bed is Asphalt `#A8A49C`); the one part that was not is the solid Skyroot deck on the snow road's north end, a Concrete part. It becomes Asphalt, RoadGravel. Spec: the Skyroot deck material and colour equal the town street's (`WorldLayout.RoadMaterial`, `TownGround.RoadColor`), and the terrain sample along the snow road inside the bed is that material too.
7. **T415 proofs.** Before shots from the base state (art identical to c423050, overlay off) and after shots from this branch into `preview/t416/`: `town-eye`, `town-top`, `road-eye-phone` (667x375) and `bayou-far`, plus a before/after pair per item (top-down, eye level, phone crop where the item shows). Only pairs that differ byte for byte are committed; identical ones are listed as unchanged in the PR body. Counts: `TownBuild.Build()` parts, models and instances on main and on the branch, and each shop interior against `ShopKit.Budget`.

## Measurable here and Studio-only

Measurable in Lune: part colours, materials, sizes, clear heights, steps, counts, source scans. Not measurable here: Studio lighting, whether a log slides off the stepped edge, truck wheels over the treads, how the faceted rock reads at game scale, lantern glow, streaming. Renders are three.js previews, not Roblox lighting, and the preview tool cannot load uploaded Roblox meshes.
